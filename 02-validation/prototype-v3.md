## What it tests

_Tie it back to the validation brief: which assumption does this prototype put in front of a user?_

The CRM doesn't fit how reps actually work, so they never adopt it.

## Context injected (no placeholders)

- **Real user quotes on screen:** “It's faster to keep my deals in a spreadsheet than to fight the CRM's eight required fields.” / “I never know where the thing I need lives. Every screen looks like a settings page.”
- **Domain metrics on screen:** 18% Weekly active adoption across licensed seats. / 8 Required fields to log a single deal update. / 11 min Avg. task time for "update a deal" vs. 2-min target.

## Iteration log (v1 → v3)

| Version | Change | Why |
|---|---|---|
| v1 | This created a visual way for sales reps to log activities | They were keeping all of their activities on spreadsheets that were not trackable |
| v2 | This augmented the UI to make it easier to use for sales resp | Adoption was low and sales reps were not abandoning their spreadsheets |
| v3 | This prioritized the daily tasks and added a team goal tracker | This focused the tasks for sales reps and connected them to their overall team so their efforts would be recognized |
