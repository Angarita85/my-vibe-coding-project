## The build

- **What I built:** A customer relationship manager can log customer interactions as calls, voicemails, notes as follow ups.
- **Tool used:** Loveable
- **Shareable link:** https://swift-crm-mate.lovable.app

## Show & Swap read

_What a partner understood from your build with no verbal setup, their reaction is your first piece of product evidence._

- **What they understood immediately:** That's is a CRM to follow up with clients, and record what happened during the communication
- **What confused them:** Why the info of the person you select appears at the bottom and does not expand on the place you select the person
- **The assumption they thought you were testing:** Maybe if it worked or not...
- **The gap between what you intended and what they read:** I think the overall understanding was there but it took a while to get there. I think it might need more context and perhaps guided steps.
